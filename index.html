About Our App

Discover the world of beers with Hoppy Center, your ultimate companion for beer enthusiasts and casual drinkers alike. Whether you're a seasoned connoisseur or just starting your beer journey, our intuitive app makes it easy to explore, discover, and share amazing brews from around the globe.

How The App Works?

Sign-Up and Onboarding

Create your account and complete the onboarding process to personalize your experience. Set your beer preferences and get started on your craft beer journey.

Scan

Use your phone’s camera to scan the barcode of any beer. Instantly access detailed info, ratings, and tasting notes to enhance your drinking experience.

Photo Sharing

Capture and share photos of your favorite beers with friends. Get likes and comments, and engage with the community through a personalized feed.

Beer Collection

Build your beer collection by logging each brew you try, populate an interactive world map with their origins, and create a wishlist of beers to explore next.
